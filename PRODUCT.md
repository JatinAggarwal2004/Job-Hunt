# Product

## Register
product

## Platform
web

## Users
People looking for openings across multiple roles and company sizes.

## Product Purpose
Job-Hunter brings scheduled discovery, resume feedback, and reviewed application preparation into one workspace. Users decide whether to accept suggested resume edits.

## Positioning
Make broad job discovery and preparing applications easier, while showing source coverage and keeping users in control.

## Brand Personality
Premium, calm, alive.

## Design Principles
Make the next action clear. Explain scores and suggestions. Label illustrative data. Keep expressive storytelling on the landing page and quick feedback in working screens. Do not promise universal coverage or autofill.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive mobile-first web, WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion alternatives.
